Product Plan

Australian Payroll Association

A lead-capture CRM — consulting, training & membership

What we're actually building and why

After this exists, every consulting, training, or membership inquiry that hits apa.com.au becomes an owned, tracked deal the moment it lands — so I can see it, work it to won or lost, and never again lose a warm lead to an email notification that no one followed up on.

Who it's for: me as the operator (and the select team who work leads), plus the person on the other side of the form — a payroll or finance leader reaching out about consulting, membership, training, or a general question.

Deliberately NOT building: no affiliates, no subscriptions, no cohorts, no analytics dashboards, no integrations beyond what's named here. If it isn't load-bearing for capturing and working a lead, it's out.

The four parts of the CRM

People
Contact directory, one row per person, deduplicated by email. Custom attributes live in attributes (jsonb).
Contacts
Inquiry pipeline. Each links to a person and moves through new_lead → contacted → discovery_call → proposal → won / lost. type constrained to consulting, membership, training, general.
activity_log
Every status change on a Contacts row writes one row here.
Orders
What people bought — product, amount, currency, status.
Newsletter
Not a table. Tracked by people.ok_to_contact = true.

Build 1 (small) — Prove the loop

Build 1 · Smallest thing that proves it works

Goal

A stranger can submit an inquiry on my live site and I can see that lead inside /admin, on the same day, without anyone touching the database by hand. This closes the exact gap in the interview. Nothing else matters until this is real.

Scope

The People and Contacts tables with custom attributes wired into the jsonb column; a working contact form on the live marketing site that writes a People row (upserted by email) and a linked Contacts row; one admin login with a single verified account; one admin page that lists incoming leads newest first.

Definition of Done
  • The contact form is live on my real domain, not localhost.
  • Submitting it creates exactly one People row and one linked Contacts row, deduplicated by email on repeat submits.
  • My chosen custom attributes are saved correctly inside attributes.
  • A new Contacts row lands in status new_lead.
  • I can log in to /admin with my one seeded account.
  • The admin leads page shows the submission within seconds, newest first.
  • I personally run the full flow once: submit as a visitor, log in, see it.
Success Criteria
  • From a cold start, I can go from submit to visible in under 60 seconds.
  • Two submissions from the same email produce one person, not two.
  • I can read the lead's name, type, message, and custom attributes on the admin page without opening Supabase.
  • No lead can land and go unseen — the failure mode from the interview.

Build 2 (all) — The system I run the business from

Build 2 · Make it the real workspace

Goal

Turn the proven loop into the place I actually manage relationships and money. I work leads, record what people bought, and keep my newsletter list entirely from /admin behind my login, and every new lead gets an automatic confirmation email. This is what makes the 90-day win achievable.

Scope

The rest of the /admin back end behind my login: the full People directory, all inquiries with working pipeline stages, the Orders list, and the Newsletter list (ok_to_contact = true). Plus Resend wired so a confirmation email fires on form submit. Every Contacts status change writes an activity_log row.

Definition of Done
  • All four parts (People, Contacts, Orders, Newsletter) are visible and usable in /admin, and all of /admin sits behind my login.
  • I can move a Contacts row through new_lead → contacted → discovery_call → proposal → won / lost from the interface.
  • Each status change writes one activity_log row with from_status, to_status, and actor.
  • The People directory is searchable and shows my custom attributes.
  • I can add an Orders row against a person and see it on their record.
  • The Newsletter list shows everyone with ok_to_contact = true.
  • Resend is connected, the sending domain is verified, and a real confirmation email arrives after a form submit.
Success Criteria
  • I can run a lead from first inquiry to won without leaving /admin or touching the database.
  • A person's full history (inquiries, status changes, and orders) is visible in one place.
  • A test submission produces a confirmation email in the inbox, not spam, with my domain as the sender.
  • Nothing in /admin is reachable without logging in.
  • At ~20 inquiries a week, this keeps up without me dropping to the database by hand.
